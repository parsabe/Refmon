## What v1.2 changed (and what it did not)

v1.2 changed no decision: all 635 variants have the same block, hold or allow in v1.1 and v1.2, and the 46 golden files give the same `decision`, `rule_decision` and failed questions as v1.0. It changed what the Gate does:

| | v1.0 | v1.1 | v1.2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| blocks that act on nothing (F6) | 14 | 10 | **0** |
| blocks after which a process is still paused (F5) | 0 | 69 | **6** |
| ... of which a launcher (a channel role) | 0 | 57 | **0** |
| holds that pause nothing | 65 | 0 | 2 |

The two holds that pause nothing are S05 typed through the run dialog to a trusted host: the only process behind the signals is the launcher, and a channel role is never touched by the fallback (A51). That is the price of leaving the launcher alone. The six blocks that still leave something paused leave eight processes, every one of which carried a label earlier (four web workers, two app servers, two `other` processes): they belong to the attack, are not channel roles, and stay paused.

The golden catalog: S05 now declares the pause it gets (F1, v1.1); the transient hold on the launcher that v1.1 added to S32 to S35 is gone.

## F8, found while evaluating (not fixed)

One variant is worse in v1.1 than in v1.0 (S02 timing=minutes: block in v1.0, effective hold in v1.1, log-odds -3.72 to -3.96, block threshold -3.91). The cause is older than v1.1. The strength of a signal is the strength of the label behind it while that label is at or above the origin threshold, and 1.0 once the label has fallen below it, with no Origin signal. A fading label therefore first weakens the evidence (strength 0.69) and then restores it (1.0) when it drops under 0.5. In v1.0 the label of this variant had dropped below 0.5 when the persistence happened, so the effect counted 2.5; in v1.1 the label carries Contract evidence and is frozen at 0.69, so the effect counts 2.5 x 0.69 plus Origin 0.8 x 0.69, which is 0.2 nats less. The calculus is not monotone in label strength. A fix (strength of a signal never below the strength of the label, or a floor of 1.0 for a labeled actor) is a change to C11.4 and would need its own seed.
