# v1.3 evaluation: v1.0, v1.1, v1.2 and v1.3 on the fourth variant seed, malicious and benign

Seed **654514256**, recorded in [variant-seeds.md](variant-seeds.md) before any v1.3 code was written, together with the rules that label a benign variant. The 879 variants (633 from the 27 malicious golden files, 246 from the 19 benign ones) were generated once (`python scripts/generate_variants.py --golden tests/golden --seed 654514256`, sha256 of the file `5c3fcbebea616806a8a9de877679d0587441d7cff11d9939b3b1b8cb49927134`; regenerating gives the same bytes) and the same file was replayed in the tags `v1.0`, `v1.1`, `v1.2` and in v1.3 by one script ([scripts/dump_matrix.py](../scripts/dump_matrix.py)); [scripts/compare_versions.py](../scripts/compare_versions.py) hashes the event streams of the four runs and found them identical. The per-variant results are in [docs/evaluation/](evaluation/).

Malicious variants: **block**; **effective hold** (the Gate paused or quarantined something); **uncontained** = allow + a hold that pauses nothing. Benign variants: **allow**, **hold**, **block**. A benign variant expects the decision of its unmodified file (allow; S49 and S50 are holds by design) unless the generator labelled its mutation, before any replay, as adding a Contract violation or an Effect (rules in [variant-seeds.md](variant-seeds.md)); the **neutral** variants are the false-alarm test and the labelled ones are reported apart. One run of the v1.3 matrix on this seed was made while the generator was being finished, to see that the new columns worked; no pipeline rule and no label rule was changed afterwards.

879 variants, identical event streams in every run (hash checked).

## What v1.3 changed

**F8 is repaired.** The two malicious variants that were worse in v1.1 and v1.2 than in v1.0 (S02 timing=minutes and S02 combo-4: block in v1.0, hold in v1.1 and v1.2) are blocks again: no malicious variant is weaker in v1.3 than in v1.0 (v1.1 and v1.2: two). Between v1.2 and v1.3, 19 holds became blocks and one allow became a hold, and none got weaker. The property that states the repair (for the same events, a label that fades faster never gives a story a higher log-odds) is tested on random streams in tests/test_v13.py: it fails on the v1.2 source (counterexamples found) and passes on v1.3, under 100 more Hypothesis seeds.

## Benign variants

The headline number is the false block rate on the 151 neutral variants: **3 of 151 (2.0%), the same three in every version**, and 5 neutral variants (the three blocks and two holds) are worse than their unmodified file, also the same in every version. v1.1, v1.2 and v1.3 did not add a false alarm to a neutral variant; the one neutral variant that changed in v1.3 (S49 combo-4, allow to hold) is a file whose own expected decision is hold.

The five are one finding, **F10**: S37 (a signed installer from the university portal, a trusted source) and S24 (a deployment tool) are allowed because the trusted-source override covers the whole story. When the steps of the install are further apart than the story quiet window (10 minutes) the persistence step is judged on its own story, without the trusted flag, and `package_installer` has `may_persist: false`: S37 timing=beyond_window, combo-1 and combo-9 block (Contract and Effect, log-odds -0.91), and S24 timing=beyond_window and combo-9 hold. The override does not survive the story window. Recorded and not fixed.

The variants that were labelled as adding a Contract violation (55) are mostly blocks, as they should be (a web worker running programs, an interactive shell running external content, a connection outside the role's list): 30, 32, 32 and 34 blocks in v1.0 to v1.3. The 17 that stay allowed are of two kinds, both by design. Twelve are an external pasted command run by another role (S11, S30, S36 and S38 through a terminal, run dialog, file manager, script host or scheduled task): Origin and the external-content Contract signal alone are 1.8 nats, L = -5.11, which is the verdict of S11 itself (a developer copying a documented command), so without an Effect the story is allowed. Five are S09 (a package from the official index, a trusted source) with the connection moved to an unknown or internal host: the trusted-root override allows the story whatever it then connects to. Both are limits of the specification and are listed in docs/results.md. The 40 labelled as adding an Effect (a hidden or obfuscated command, a connection to an unknown host) are holds, 35 of them in v1.3, and 5 blocks.
